## Proxy types

| Type | What it means | Common billing | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Datacenter | Hosting-provider IP ranges | IP/month or GB | Speed and high volume where accepted |
| Rotating residential | Household IP pool with rotating/sticky sessions | GB, request or IP | Geo testing and public data collection |
| ISP/static residential | ISP-associated address, usually served from server infrastructure | IP/month or GB | Stable sessions and account workflows |
| Mobile | Carrier IP, often behind CGNAT | GB, port/hour/day/month | Mobile-first targets and carrier testing |
| Scraping API | Provider runs proxy, rendering and retries | Request, result, credit or GB | Buy structured output instead of operating a proxy layer |

HTTP(S), SOCKS5 and UDP describe protocols; they do not prove the source IP is residential. “Unlimited” may still have fair-use, speed or concurrency limits.

### Package conditions worth checking

- **SocksNode:** mobile 5 GB is $19.50; 100 GB is $260; 2 TB is $4,710.40. State/city/ASN targeting counts traffic **2×**, while country targeting uses the base rate. Economy US is $30/30 days and Basic $40/30 days, with 15 GB/day included. This is not unlimited traffic.
- **SocksNode datacenter:** 1 GB is $0.66; 10 GB $5.70; 100 GB $49; 10 TB $2,969.60. The displayed calculation uses 1 TB = 1,024 GB. The static ISP menu and homepage differ: confirm the country and rental period at checkout.
- **Evomi:** the $0.30/GB datacenter rate applies to 1,000 GB. Confirm PAYG minimums. The $1/IP static residential offer is shared and subscription-based, not automatically dedicated.
- **Oxylabs:** Starter is $30/month. Residential at $6/GB provides up to 5 GB if credits are used exclusively for that product. Flexible is $500/month with residential at $4/GB. Starter ISP is $1.50/IP, dedicated ISP $3/IP and dedicated DC $2/IP. Starter/Flexible datacenter rates are $0.60/$0.45 per GB; mobile rates are $7.50/$5 per GB. Do not count the same credits twice across products.
- **SOAX:** Builder includes 200 credits, with $3/$2.25/$1.20 per GB across geo tiers. Residential and mobile share the geo-tier rate system; the exit IP country determines the tier.
- **IPRoyal:** advertised per-IP prices do not establish the rental period or minimum. Do not silently relabel them as IP/month.
- **Proxy-Cheap:** [ISP Basic](https://www.proxy-cheap.com/) was displayed from $2.71/month, shared by up to three users with 1 GB included in the reviewed configuration.
- **Proxy-Seller:** [headline prices](https://proxy-seller.com/) were IPv4 from $0.49, ISP from $0.98 and IPv6 from $0.02; country and duration were not established. Do not rank them as IP/month prices.
- **Rayobyte:** static datacenter starts at $0.85/IP/month; distinguish dedicated from semi-dedicated and confirm minimum quantities. Scale products and Premium residential need separate evaluation.

## Cost analysis

For 100 GB residential, the public examples are: Evomi monthly $49; Evomi PAYG $99; SocksNode prepaid $73; PacketStream $100; SOAX Builder Tier 1 about $300 including the plan and extra credits shown by its calculator. These figures exclude tax and payment fees and measure invoice cost, not successful results.

```text
effective cost per successful result
= (proxy + API/solver + compute + retry cost) / valid results
```

If only 10 GB of an Evomi $49 monthly plan is used, the effective rate is $4.90/GB consumed. Unused prepaid traffic may remain available for future months, so distinguish cash outlay from usage cost.

Hypothetical example: A costs $20 for 80,000 valid results ($0.25/1K); B costs $15 for 40,000 ($0.375/1K). A has the larger invoice but the lower cost per valid result. This is arithmetic, not a provider benchmark.

Include retries, two-way traffic, geo multipliers, sticky-session loss, concurrency limits, expiry, refunds and fair-use rules. More credentials do not mean independent exit IPs. Managed APIs may charge multiple credits for rendering or premium targets. A cheaper GB can be more expensive per successful result.

<a id="benchmark"></a>
## Benchmark checklist

1. Fix the authorized workload, URL, country, request size and success criteria.
2. Test the same concurrency and time window across providers.
3. Record p50/p95 latency, timeout, HTTP status, content validity and dashboard cost.
4. Check ASN, actual country, DNS/WebRTC and sticky-session behavior.
5. Repeat at different times; one demo page is not a network benchmark.
6. Scale purchases only after cost per valid result and support meet your needs.

Use [BENCHMARK.md](BENCHMARK.md) to record measurements; write `not measured` instead of copying vendor claims.
